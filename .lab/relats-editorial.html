01 — Challenge

Engineering ahead of its image.

Relats has manufactured thermal and electrical protection since 1956, supplying the most demanding industries on five continents. Yet its brand read like a commodity supplier, not the materials-science leader it is.

The gap was costing it presence in premium, innovation-led conversations.

02 — Strategy

Position the science, not the product.

We reframed Relats around a single idea — ahead of the curve — built on six decades of materials know-how. A clear architecture connected automotive, industrial and aerospace under one confident story.

03 — Identity & Digital

A precise, modern system.

A tightened wordmark, an industrial-red palette and a technical type system gave Relats a contemporary, engineered feel — carried into a fast, scalable website that finally matches the products inside.
