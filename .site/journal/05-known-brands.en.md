# Why B2B buyers choose the brand they already know

**Short answer:** because most of the decision is made before anyone calls you. Research from the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute suggests that only around 5% of business buyers are in the market for a given product at any one time. When their moment comes, they shortlist the names they already know and trust. A brand is how you get on that list before the tender, the RFQ or the trade fair.

Many industrial companies put everything into the moment of sale: fairs, quotes, discounts, visits. That moment matters. But by then, most buyers already have two or three names in mind.

## The 95–5 rule

Companies don’t change suppliers every month. They do it every few years, when a contract ends, a plant opens or a problem appears. That is why Professor John Dawes of the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute concluded that around 95% of B2B buyers are out of the market at any given time (*The 95-5 Rule*, LinkedIn B2B Institute, 2021).

If your communication only speaks to the 5% buying now, you are invisible to the 95% who will buy next year.

## Buyers research on their own

Gartner found that B2B buyers spend only 17% of their buying time meeting potential suppliers. The rest goes on research: websites, catalogues, LinkedIn, colleagues and, more and more, AI assistants. If your brand doesn’t explain you clearly when you are not in the room, a competitor’s will.

## What “known” means in B2B

It doesn’t mean famous. It means three things:

1. **Easy to recall** when the need appears.
2. **Clearly linked to one thing.** “The covering specialist for e-mobility” beats “a reliable supplier since 1965”.
3. **Easy to check.** The website, the cases and the people confirm what the buyer has heard.

## What to do about it

- **One clear idea.** A positioning the whole company can repeat.
- **A consistent identity.** The same brand on the stand, the catalogue, the fleet and the website, so every contact adds up.
- **Show up between purchases.** Cases, articles, trade press and LinkedIn: useful things, regularly.
- **Be findable by AI.** Buyers ask assistants for suppliers. Clear pages with real answers get cited. See [how AI assistants choose](/en/journal/geo-ai-assistants-b2b/).

## FAQ

**Isn’t word of mouth enough in our sector?** It works until something changes: a new purchasing manager, a new market, a new generation. Then the brand is what they find.

**We sell to engineers. Do they care about brand?** Engineers decide on specifications, but they shortlist by reputation, and they check your website before they call. A clear brand makes the technical case easier to read.
