The problem

Many companies reach a point where marketing needs a leader, not another supplier. Agencies execute, the team is junior or stretched, and the CEO ends up making marketing decisions in between everything else. Hiring a senior CMO takes months, costs a lot and is a big bet when you’re not yet sure what the role should be.

In one line
You don’t need more marketing. You need someone to lead it.
Signs it’s time

Three signs you need a CMO — part-time.

01

The CEO is the marketing department

Every campaign, every agency brief and every website change ends up on the CEO’s desk. Decisions come late, in a hurry and without a plan behind them.

02

Lots of activity, no direction

Social posts, trade fairs, ads, a new brochure. Each one makes sense; together they don’t add up to a brand, a pipeline or a story buyers remember.

03

Too early for a full-time CMO

You need senior judgement now, but not yet a full-time senior salary — or you want to define the role properly before you recruit for it.

How we work

From diagnosis to hand-over — leadership that leaves a team behind.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    2–4 weeks

    Interviews with management, sales and marketing, one workshop and a deliverable session: where you are, how you are seen and what to fix first.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Ongoing

    Positioning, priorities, budget and KPIs in one clear plan everyone understands — reviewed as the results come in.

  3. 03

    Lead

    Ongoing

    Part-time, hands-on leadership of positioning, brand, website, performance and the team — agencies and suppliers briefed clearly and held to the plan. With the same senior people who build the brand.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    When you hire

    When it’s time for your own CMO, we help define the role and hand over the plan, the brand and the team — so they start with a system that runs, not a blank page.

FAQ

What CEOs ask us about a fractional CMO.

How does a fractional CMO work day to day?

Part-time, senior and hands-on. We set the positioning and the plan, lead brand, website and performance, coordinate agencies and suppliers, and work alongside your team — with the same senior people who build the brand. Cesc Callejas, with two decades on both sides of the table, leads it personally. Until you hire your own CMO; then we hand over.

What does the process look like?

Strategy: initial interviews, one workshop and a deliverable session. Brand: time to work, progress sessions and a final presentation. Then UX/UI, design and development, showing you the progress until the result is live. Cesc Callejas leads every project personally.

Do you build on WordPress or Webflow?

No. Templates never quite fit and become expensive to change. We build custom, lightweight websites with simple maintenance — and a custom back office when you need one, which is much faster to work with.

Do you work outside Catalonia?

Yes. We are based in Barcelona and work in Catalan, Spanish and English with companies across Spain and Europe, and with brands that sell worldwide.

Is your brand behind your company?

Tell us where you are. We’ll tell you honestly whether a brand project makes sense now.
