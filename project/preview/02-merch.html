The Infinit® mark behaves the same across paper, fabric and aluminium. Open mark on light, solid on dark, Studio Blue when the surface allows colour. Every imprint sits at cap-height of the wordmark. The aim isn't swag — it's seven small surfaces that read as one system.

— Production notes

Each surface holds a single imprint. Mark height = 1.6× cap-height of the wordmark. Use the open mark on warm surfaces, the solid mark on dark, and Studio Blue when the surface allows colour. No additional taglines beyond "designed to scale." — kept editorial, in Source Serif italic.
